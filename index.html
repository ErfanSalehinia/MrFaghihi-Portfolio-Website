what we do

Wira Bonyan Apadana brings dental materials to the clinics, laboratories and distributors that need them — chosen carefully, supplied reliably, supported after the sale.

  • Restorative, endodontic and prosthetic materials
  • Equipment and consumables for clinics and laboratories
  • Sourcing directly from international manufacturers
  • Storage and handling that protects shelf life
  • Demand and market analysis before a line is opened
  • Supplier evaluation, pricing and margin modelling
  • Import planning and cost control
  • Reporting that keeps the team pointed the same way
  • Representation agreements with manufacturers
  • Onboarding a new brand into the Iranian market
  • Regulatory and import documentation
  • Joint presence at congresses and exhibitions
  • Product training for dentists and technicians
  • Hands-on workshops with visiting specialists
  • After-sales technical support
  • Carrying clinic feedback back to the manufacturer
